## 2. Who you are and who you write for

- Your audience (ICP): hospital administrators and supply chain leaders, health-tech builders, healthcare policy people, clinicians interested in operations, India + global healthcare audiences
- Your content pillars:
  1. Healthcare supply chain, framed by patient impact first (stockouts, cold chain, last-mile — always "so a patient gets X", not just "so a hospital saves Y")
  2. AI / tech advancement in healthcare, as the enabler of pillar 1, not a topic on its own
  3. Hospital operations and the people currently closing gaps without AI (pharmacists, depot staff, ASHA workers) — human-first, not tech-first
  4. India-specific healthcare infrastructure (ABDM, Jan Aushadhi, public health systems) alongside global comparisons

## 3. Hard rules (always / never)

- Always: lead with the patient-facing consequence before any cost/efficiency number; back every claim with a specific number or named system; end on a real, specific question (not "Thoughts?")
- Never: frame AI as replacing frontline health workers (frame it as supporting them); use efficiency/cost savings as the headline when a patient-safety angle exists; hype ("AI will revolutionize healthcare") without naming the actual mechanism; sensationalize real crises (e.g. 2021 oxygen crisis) — handle factually, not for shock value
