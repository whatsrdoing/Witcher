ACCOUNT PHOTOS
==============

Two ways to give an account a picture. Either works; you do not need both.

1. FROM THE APP  (easiest)
   Sign in, click your name in the top-right, then "Add photo".
   The picture is squared off, scaled to 512px and saved into the
   data folder next to index.html, alongside everything else you
   upload. It survives clearing the browser and follows the account
   to any browser on this machine.

2. DROP A FILE IN THIS FOLDER
   Name it after the username, lower-case, with every character
   that is not a letter or a number turned into a dash:

       admin/ritik   ->   admin-ritik.png

   PNG, JPG and WEBP all work. Square images look best; anything
   else is centre-cropped to a circle. It is picked up on the next
   page load.

If both exist, the one chosen in the app wins — an explicit choice
made just now should beat a file that has been sitting here since
install. Remove it from the account menu to fall back to the file.

With no picture at all the account shows its initials.
